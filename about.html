How to Use

The tool has two connected parts: a scatterplot for exploring article relationships, and a Wikipedia Map for visualizing how those articles link to each other.

Scatterplot Controls

  • X / Y Axis Dropdowns: Change the dimensions plotted on each axis (e.g. different PCA components or embedding dimensions).
  • Embedding Model: Switch between Base Model, Instruct Model, and BERT Embeddings to change how articles are positioned in the space.
  • Scoring Dropdown: Color the dots by Article Groups, Groups + Base Influence, or Groups + Instruct Influence to highlight different relationships.
  • View Mode (Sections / Articles): Toggle between showing individual article sections as points, or collapsing them into single article-level points.
  • Hover: Hover over any dot to see the article title. In Articles view, hovering highlights all dots belonging to the same article group.
  • Click (in Articles view): Click a group dot to zoom into that article group and see its individual sections.

Opening the Wikipedia Map

Note: We recommend adding no more than 10 articles at a time to the Wikipedia Map. Beyond that, the graph can become difficult to read and takes significantly longer to load.

  • Hover + Space: While hovering over any dot, press Space to send that article to the Wikipedia Map. The map opens in a new tab (or updates the existing one).
  • Adding more articles: Each subsequent hover + Space adds another article to the same map session. All previously added articles are preserved.
  • Auto-expansion: Articles are automatically expanded to show their sections and linked articles as nodes in the graph.
  • Gold highlighted paths: Once expansion finishes, the shortest connecting paths between all root articles are highlighted in gold, showing how the topics relate through Wikipedia's link structure.

Wikipedia Map Interactions

  • Click an unexpanded node: Expands it to show its sections or linked articles.
  • Click an already-expanded node: Starts a manual path selection. Click a second expanded node to highlight the shortest path between them in gold.
  • Hover a node: Highlights the traceback path from that node to its root article.
  • Expansion modes: Use the mode dropdown to switch between Mode A (sections with shapes), Mode B (sections then links), and Mode C (sections with colors).
